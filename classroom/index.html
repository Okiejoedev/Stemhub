About this demo

A 2026 browser re-creation inspired by my BSc final-year project, Interactive 3D Classroom with User Support Using Virtual Reality (Federal University Oye-Ekiti, supervised by Mrs. Daramola). The original was built with Blender, Unity, Unreal Engine and Mozilla Spoke and tested with 50 students and 5 educators. Its files were not preserved.

This version is new work. The courses, rooms, models, live classes, board uploads and class monitoring were designed for this demo and are not copies of the original.

  • Four courses, each with a lecture hall, a lab, a library and a discussion room, opening onto shared school grounds.
  • Lecturers can put a PDF, slide images or a video on the lecture-hall board; students’ boards follow the lecturer’s page, and videos play in sync. YouTube links are supported too.
  • Separate sign-in for lecturers and students; lecturer accounts need the school’s invite code.
  • School grounds with a football pitch and a playground, reached through each room’s back door.
  • Live classes: students join with a code; the lecturer sees each student’s room, activity, whether the class tab is open, and raised hands.
  • Students are told what the lecturer can see.
  • The simulated class preview uses made-up students to show the lecturer’s view.

Put your material on the board

Choose a PDF, one or more slide images (PNG or JPG), or a video (MP4 or WebM, up to 50 MB). Each PDF page or image becomes one slide. A video plays on the lecture-hall screen.

Using PowerPoint or Google Slides? Save or download the presentation as a PDF first (in PowerPoint: File → Save As → PDF), then choose that file.
