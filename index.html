Two working prototypes, built in October 2026. Both run in the browser on a laptop or phone, with no sign-in needed to try them.

Personal health informatics

Wristside

Answers questions about a person’s own wearable data, such as “How did I sleep this week?”, with every number calculated from the records and gaps in the data named.

  • Layered answers: the answer, then a comparison, then context.
  • A smartwatch view of the short answer, and a read-aloud option.
  • A chart and a list of the records behind each answer.
  • Uses generated sample data for one fictional person.

Learning technology · 3D interfaces

Interactive 3D Classroom

A browser re-creation inspired by my 2023 BSc final-year VR classroom (Federal University Oye-Ekiti), whose original files were not preserved. The rooms, models and live-class features here are new work.

  • Four courses, each with a lecture hall, a lab with a 3D model, a library and a discussion room.
  • Lecturers can put a PDF or slide images on the board.
  • Live classes with join codes and a lecturer view of where students are and what they are doing; students are told what the lecturer can see.
  • A simulated class preview shows the lecturer’s view with made-up students.
